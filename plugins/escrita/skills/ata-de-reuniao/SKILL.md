# Meeting minutes

Build the minutes like this:

**Meeting:** subject · date · who took part

1. **Summary** (3 to 5 lines): what was discussed and the outcome.
2. **Decisions:** each decision on one line.
3. **Tasks:** a table with *What*, *Who* and *Due date*. If the owner or the deadline wasn't stated, write "to be defined"; don't invent one.
4. **Open items:** what was left open for the next meeting.

Rules:
- Use the words from the notes; don't add what nobody said.
- If the transcript has confusing passages, mark them with [unintelligible passage] instead of guessing.
- At the end, offer a short email to send the minutes to the participants.
