# Proofread a text

1. Fix spelling, accents, agreement, grammar and punctuation, following the rules of the language the text is written in. For Portuguese, follow Brazilian Portuguese (including regência and crase).
2. Improve clarity only where it gets in the way of reading: a sentence that is too long, a repeated word, a confusing order.
3. **Do not change** the meaning, the opinion or the person's way of writing. Slang and informality that are on purpose stay.
4. Deliver:
   - the complete proofread text, ready to copy;
   - then a short list of the most important changes (before → after), so the person can learn from them and check.
5. If something looks like an error of content (date, number, name), point it out instead of fixing it on your own.
