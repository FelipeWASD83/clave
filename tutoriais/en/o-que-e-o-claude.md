Claude is an [artificial intelligence](#/aprender/glossario/ia) made by a company called Anthropic. You write, it answers, like a conversation with a very well-read and patient person who is available at any hour.

## What it does well

- **Explain** any subject, the way you need it: simpler, with examples, step by step.
- **Write and proofread** texts: emails, school papers, posts, résumés, simple contracts.
- **Read and summarize** files: PDFs, spreadsheets, photos of documents, screenshots.
- **Organize ideas**: lists, plans, outlines, comparison tables.
- **Create things**: Excel spreadsheets, web pages, small programs.
- **Automate tasks on your computer**, in Code mode: organize folders, rename files, merge spreadsheets.

```experimentar
Explain what you can do for me, with 5 practical examples for someone who works in a small office.
```

## What it isn't

- **It isn't a person**, and it doesn't "know" things the way we do: it learned patterns by reading a huge amount of text. Sometimes it **makes up** a piece of information with total confidence (this has a name: [hallucination](#/aprender/glossario/alucinacao)). For anything important, ask for the source and double-check.
- **It doesn't know what happened yesterday** off the top of its head: for that it searches the web, which comes on (the **Web** button in the text box turns it off in a chat).
- **It doesn't remember other conversations**, unless you turn on [memory](#/aprender/memoria).
- **It doesn't replace** a doctor, lawyer or accountant for a serious decision. It helps you understand and prepare your questions.

## Where your messages go

What you write and attach goes to Anthropic, which processes it and sends back the answer. Your conversations are stored **on your computer**, inside Clave. Avoid sending passwords, card numbers and other people's documents unless you really need to.

## How to talk to it

Write the way you would talk to a person: give the context, what you want, and how you want the answer. If you don't like it, ask for changes ("shorter", "more formal", "explain again with an example"): it never gets tired. The lesson [How to ask well](#/aprender/como-pedir-bem) shows the tricks.

```experimentar
I own a small bakery. Help me reply to a customer who complained that her order was late, politely, and offer her 10% off her next order.
```
