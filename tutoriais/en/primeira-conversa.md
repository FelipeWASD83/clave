## Send a message

Click **New chat** (or press **Ctrl + Shift + O**), write your message and press **Enter**. To start a new line without sending, use **Shift + Enter**. While Claude is answering, the send button turns into a square: click it (or press **Esc**) to stop.

```experimentar
Give me 5 birthday gift ideas for a mom who loves gardening, with approximate prices in dollars.
```

## Attach files

Click the paperclip, drag the file into the text box, or paste an image (Ctrl + V). Claude can read:

- **Photos and screenshots** (PNG, JPG): "what does this photo say?", "what's wrong with this screen?"
- **PDFs**: "summarize this contract and tell me the penalties".
- **Excel spreadsheets** (.xlsx): "which months did I spend the most?". See the lesson [Excel with Claude](#/aprender/excel).
- **Text and code**: .txt, .csv, .md, .py and others.

## Didn't like the answer?

- **Ask for changes** in the same conversation: "shorter", "in bullet points", "more formal".
- **Generate another answer**: hover over the answer and click the little circular arrow. You can pick a different model.
- **Edit your message**: hover over your bubble and click the pencil. Claude answers again from that point.

When different versions exist, **< 1/2 >** arrows appear so you can switch between them. Nothing is lost.

## Old conversations

They live in the left sidebar, sorted by date. Use **search** (Ctrl + K) to find one by title or by content. In each conversation's menu (the three dots) you can **pin**, **rename**, **export** as Markdown or **delete**.

## The gauge and the cost

Below the text box you'll see the context gauge (how much of the conversation's "memory" has been used) and how much this conversation has cost so far. Each answer shows its model and its cost.

```experimentar
Make a table with 3 columns (day, meal, ingredients) for a simple one-week menu.
```
