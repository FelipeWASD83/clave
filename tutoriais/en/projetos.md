A **project** gathers conversations about the same subject and gives them two things that Claude always reads:

- **Instructions**: how to work on that subject.
- **Knowledge files**: documents it should consult (PDFs, spreadsheets, texts).

## When to use it

- **Thesis or college coursework**: instructions with the topic, the formatting rules (APA, MLA...) and what your advisor asks for; files with the research proposal and the main articles.
- **Shop or business**: instructions with the brand's tone and the customer service rules; files with the price list and the returns policy.
- **Channel or social media**: instructions with the audience and the style of your videos; files with the scripts that worked.

## How to create one

1. In the left sidebar, click **Projects** and then **New project**.
2. Give it a name and write the instructions as if you were explaining the job to a new assistant.
3. Add the files (from your computer or from GitHub).
4. Click **New chat in project**.

Example instructions:

> This project is my cake shop on Instagram (@example). Audience: moms aged 25 to 45 in Portland. Tone: warm and direct, with at most 2 emojis. Always remind people that orders need 3 days' notice. Prices are in the file price-list.xlsx; never make up a price.

## Good to know

- Files are loaded at the start of every **new** conversation in the project. Older conversations keep the files they started with.
- A big file makes every answer more expensive (it gets reread with every message). Only send what matters.

```experimentar
Help me write the instructions for a project for my thesis. Ask me questions about my topic and what my advisor expects, and at the end give me the finished text ready to paste.
```
