Everything you write in **Settings > Personalization** is read by Claude at the start of **every new conversation**. It's the fastest way to stop repeating "keep it short", "no hard words", "I'm a teacher".

## About you

Say who you are and what Claude needs to know to get the level of its answers right:

> I'm 41, a middle school science teacher at a public school in Denver. I don't know how to code. I use Windows, Word, Excel and Google Classroom.

Good things to put here: your job, what you know and don't know, the programs you use, your city (for examples and prices), what else you use Claude for.

## How you want the answers

> Short, direct answers in plain English. Use bullet points for step-by-step instructions. If I'm wrong, tell me right at the start. No jargon when a simple word exists.

Be specific: "short" helps a little; "at most 5 lines, unless I ask for detail" helps a lot.

## Styles

A style is a way of writing, and you switch it depending on the situation:

- **Normal**: the default.
- **Concise**: gets straight to the point.
- **Explanatory**: explains like it's for someone who is learning, with examples.
- **Formal**: professional tone, good for work emails.

You can **create your own styles** in Settings > Personalization > Create style. Examples:

- "Patient teacher": explains with everyday analogies and ends with a question to check whether I understood.
- "Picky proofreader": points out every grammar and logic mistake in my text, with no compliments.

## It applies to new conversations

Personalization kicks in when a conversation **starts**. Did you change something? Start a new chat for it to take effect. (This is on purpose: it lets Clave reuse the [cache](#/aprender/glossario/cache), so the conversation costs less.)
